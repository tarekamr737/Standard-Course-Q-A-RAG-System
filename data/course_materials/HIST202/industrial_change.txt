# Week 3: The Industrial Revolution

## Why Britain?

Industrialization began in Britain through a combination of accessible coal, commercial capital, expanding markets, and institutions that supported investment. Agricultural changes also increased food production and released labor for non-agricultural work.

## Factory production

Factory production concentrated machines and wage labor in one place. It increased output in textile manufacture but often imposed long hours, rigid time discipline, and unsafe conditions. Reformers and workers organized around these conditions during the nineteenth century.

## Historical interpretation

Course materials distinguish technological change from its social consequences. A new machine did not affect all people equally; effects varied by occupation, gender, age, region, and access to property.
